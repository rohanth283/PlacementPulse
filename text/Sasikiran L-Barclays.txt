Intern Interview Experience – Barclays 
 
About the Company: 
Barclays is a British multinational bank founded in 1690 and headquartered in London, 
with operations in nearly 40 countries and serving around 48 million customers. Known 
for historic firsts—like funding the world’s first industrial steam railway and launching 
the world’s first ATM in 1967—it today oƯers services spanning retail and corporate 
banking, investment banking, and wealth management. With assets exceeding £1.5 
trillion, Barclays is doubling down on innovation: backing AI startups, driving open 
banking, and committing over $1 trillion to sustainable finance by 2030. It continues to 
cement its role as a key player in shaping the future of global finance. 
 
There were two stages to the selection process: 
 Online Assessment 
 Interviews 
Around 450 students applied, and only ~170 were shortlisted for the OA. 
Online Assessment 
 Online Assessment was 70 mins for 25 questions: 20 MCQs and 5 SQL 
queries.  
 MCQs were based on OOPS concepts, output prediction, and debugging of 
C/C++/Java, basic aptitude questions. 
 The SQL questions, while conceptually simple, were presented in long passages, 
making them time-consuming. They covered string manipulation, aggregate 
functions, nested queries/joins, and date/time extraction. 
 It was conducted on the HackerEarth platform. 
 Time management was essential to this OA. I managed to answer 19 MCQs out 
of 20 and 4 SQL queries out of 5 
46 students were shortlisted for the interview. 
 
Before the interviews started, we were shown a presentation from the Barclays team 
about its history, its presence in India and what this intern role entails. 
Interview process was split into 2 rounds. 
 
 
Round 1: Technical Interview @ CUIC 
 This round started oƯ with a self-introduction in which I explained my projects 
briefly and my interests in AI/ML. 
 Then she asked about a project that I had mentioned in my resume and asked 
me to write its schema on paper. She asked several follow up questions 
regarding the schema which I was able to answer. 
 She asked me to write a SQL query to find the second largest table in employee 
table which I did using nested query. 
 She asked how to implement concurrency in MySQL and MongoDB. 
 She asked me to design a URL Shortener, a system design question I wasn’t 
prepared for. I explained how I’d approach it using the MERN stack, describing 
how shortened URLs would be generated and mapped in the database. 
 She asked how I would solve the shortest path problem and I explained using 
Dijkstra’s Algorithm and she asked me to write the pseudo code. 
 She asked me how multilevel or multiple inheritance is implemented in Java and 
I explained Java interfaces and their implementations. 
 She then asked me if I had questions and I asked how GenAI being used in 
modern fintech especially in their company. 
25 students were shortlisted for HR round. 
 
Round 2: HR Round @ CUIC 
 For me, the HR round was longer, and I performed better there than in the 
technical round and the interviewer was friendly so I was able to answer 
comfortably. 
 It started oƯ with a self-introduction in which I gave an introduction similar to 
what I did in the first round. 
 Then she moved on and asked me about one of my projects and how you would 
handle tight deadlines in it. I answered honestly, sharing my experience working 
under tight deadlines for an NGO project where we had to deliver a prototype 
within a week. 
 She asked how will I react if your plan for a project did not go according to plan 
and something broke before final submission. I answered I would try my best to 
fix it but if I couldn’t I would admit something broke in the final moment and ask 
for a small extension 
 She asked, if while working as an intern in a team, after all project details were 
finalized, I came up with a better implementation for a module, would I approach 
my project manager. I answered yes, I would but would not be discouraged if it 
was rejected because there would be a reason for it and I would learn from it. 
 She asked asked what would you do if you found out your teammate had 
plagiarized his part of a project after final submission. 
 She asked where I would see myself in 5 years and after I answered she asked 
me what about in 10 years and I answered that too. 
 She asked me if there are any questions and I asked what challenges would 
interns face during the internship. 
Out of 25 students, 15 students were finalized for Barclays 2026 Summer Internship 
Program.
